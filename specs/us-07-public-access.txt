# US-07: Public access to tap control (open/close)

**As a** festival attendee,
**I want** to open and close taps without logging in,
**so that** I can quickly get a drink without any authentication barriers.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The endpoints `PUT /dispenser/{id}/status` are publicly accessible (no authentication required).
- All other endpoints (creation, listing, spending) require admin authentication.

**Technical notes:**
- This matches the MVP requirement that attendees do not need to authenticate.
